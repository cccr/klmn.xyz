Where an opening's settings go

In Shelf House's front view, clicking a room unwraps it. Every wall's controls and openings are laid out around the room, and the view zooms to fit. But an opening's settings panel takes space, and whatever space it takes comes out of the room. Here are six ways to handle that, each measured on the live front view below.
